Pairing & help

First connection
  1. Open this page in Bluefy.
  2. Switch the G3 on (or plug in the charger). Fully close the Segway app and Scooter Hacking Utility, because only one app can hold the connection.
  3. Tap Connect and pick your scooter from the list.
  4. When the orange bar appears, press the power button once. This phone is then remembered.

If the scooter won't accept the button press, it's still bound to another app's password. Paste that password below (from SHU, or the Segway app's <SERIAL>_decrypt value in an iPhone backup).
